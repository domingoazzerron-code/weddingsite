    How we work with you

    1. Initial consultation

      Get in touch by email, WhatsApp or the brief form in the contact section below. We’ll reply to talk through your project, your editing style and the turnaround you need.

    2. Complete your creative brief

      Complete our quick creative brief so we capture your preferences, brand style and specific instructions from the start.

    3. Editing and collaboration

      Our senior editor and project manager will carefully build your first draft around your specific preferences. When ready, we’ll upload it to Frame.io so you can add direct comments and annotations right on the timeline. From there, we’ll implement your feedback to polish every detail.

    4. Finalization

      After incorporating your feedback, we’ll upload the polished cut to Frame.io for your final review. Once approved, we will lock the edit and bundle all project assets for export.

    5. Project handover

      After your final sign-off, we’ll send a PayPal payment link. Once it’s paid, we deliver your high-resolution exports and all project files via Dropbox.

    Good to know

    What should I have ready?

    A footage link (Dropbox, Google Drive or similar), the wedding date and your deadline, the deliverable you want, a few style references, and a music genre and mood. If your files are large or messy, tell us and we’ll find the easiest way to get them over. You don’t need everything on day one, and the brief asks for each of these.

    Can I try you before committing to a full project?

    Yes. New clients can request a 30–60 second test edit on their own footage. Tick “Test edit” in the brief and send a few minutes of footage. A minimal charge applies, and we confirm the amount when we reply. If you like the result, we move on to the full project.

    Can you match my editing style?

    Yes. Choose “Match my existing style” in the brief and send a few references.

    Can you use the songs I choose?

    Commercial songs are copyrighted and need a license to be used in a final film. If you have your own music account (Musicbed, Artlist or similar), send us the track and we’ll use it. If not, we provide licensed music from Musicbed in a similar style. Any songs you share guide the mood and pacing.

    How long will it take, and what does it cost?

    Typically 3–4 days for a teaser and highlight film, and 4–5 days if we also edit the ceremony and speeches, counted from the day we receive your footage. Give us your deadline in the brief and we’ll plan around it. Prices start from $85 for a teaser and $200 for a highlight film. Your exact price and timing depend on your footage and deadline, and we confirm both within a day of receiving your brief.

    How do reviews and revisions work?

    Every project includes 2–3 rounds of revisions on the agreed brief. Leave comments directly on the timeline in Frame.io and we’ll update the cut each round. If you need more rounds or want to change the brief, we’ll agree that with you first.

    What software do you use, and how do I get the files?

    We edit in Adobe Premiere Pro. Drafts go to Frame.io for your comments. After you approve and pay, we deliver the high-resolution exports and project files through Dropbox.

    Do you offer rush delivery?

    Yes, for an additional fee. Put your deadline in the brief and we’ll confirm what is possible and the cost.

    Privacy

    We keep this simple: we only collect what we need to answer you and do the work.

    What we collect

    The details you enter in the brief form (name, email, dates, project details and footage link), or anything you send us by email or chat.

    Why

    To reply to your inquiry and, if you hire us, to deliver your project. We don’t sell your details or use them for third-party marketing.

    Who handles it

    Briefs reach us by email, either through your own email app or through our form service (Formspree). Emails are handled through Gmail. Footage you share is used only to edit your project.

    Analytics

    We use Plausible, a privacy-friendly analytics tool that doesn’t use cookies or collect personal data, to count visits and clicks.

    On your device

    If you pick a light or dark theme, that choice is saved in your browser only. So is a half-finished brief, until you send it. Fonts come from Google Fonts, and film thumbnails and players come from YouTube. Both may receive your device information when they load or play.

    Email marcusweddingstudio@gmail.com any time to ask us to correct or delete your details.
