Process

How we work with you

  1. Initial consultation

    Let’s chat! Drop us a message using the contact section below so we can discuss your project requirements, editing style, and preferred turnaround time.

  2. Fill up your creative brief

    To help us bring your exact vision to life, we’ll ask you to fill out our quick creative brief. This ensures we capture all your preferences, brand style, and specific instructions right from the start.

  3. Editing and collaboration

    Our Senior Editor and Project Manager will carefully build your first draft around your specific preferences. When ready, we’ll upload it to Frame.io so you can add direct comments and annotations right on the timeline. From there, we’ll implement your feedback to polish every detail.

  4. Finalization

    After incorporating your feedback, we’ll upload the polished cut to Frame.io for your final review. Once approved, we will lock the edit and bundle all project assets for export.

  5. Project handover

    After your final sign-off, we’ll send a PayPal payment link. As soon as payment is complete, we’ll deliver your high-resolution exports along with all project files and assets directly via Dropbox.

FAQ

Good to know

How does a project work?

It starts with a quick chat about your project, then you fill out a short creative brief. We build a first draft, share it on Frame.io for your comments on the timeline, polish it with your feedback, and do a final review. After sign-off, we hand over your files.

What can you edit?

Teasers, highlight films, full-length films, and ceremony and speeches edits. If you need something else, ask and we’ll tell you if it’s a fit.

How do I send you the footage?

Share a download link (Dropbox, Google Drive or similar) in the brief, or send it when we reply. If your files are large or messy, tell us and we’ll work out the easiest way to get them over.

Can you match my editing style?

Yes. Choose “Match my existing style” in the brief and send a few of your previous films or references. If you’d rather explore, we can go cinematic or documentary, or help you decide.

How long will it take, and what does it cost?

It depends on the deliverable, how much footage there is and your deadline. Tell us your wedding date and the date you need it by, and we’ll reply within a day to confirm timing and pricing.

How do reviews and revisions work?

We upload drafts to Frame.io, where you can leave comments directly on the timeline. We apply your feedback, then send the polished cut for a final review before we lock the edit.

How do payment and delivery work?

After your final sign-off we send a PayPal payment link. Once payment is complete, we deliver your high-resolution exports, project files and assets via Dropbox.

Where are you based?

We’re a collaborative team working together in a studio in Manila, Philippines.

Privacy

We keep this simple: we only collect what we need to answer you and do the work.

What we collect

The details you enter in the brief form (name, email, dates, project details and footage link), or anything you send us by email or chat.

Why

To reply to your inquiry and, if you hire us, to deliver your project. We don’t sell your details or use them for third-party marketing.

Who handles it

Briefs go to our email through our form provider. Emails are handled through Gmail. Footage you share is used only to edit your project.

Analytics
