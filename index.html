How we work with you

  1. Initial consultation

    Get in touch in the contact section below, by email, WhatsApp or the brief form. We’ll reply within a day to talk through your project, your editing style and the turnaround you need.

  2. Fill out your creative brief

    Fill out our quick creative brief so we capture your preferences, brand style and specific instructions from the start.

  3. Editing and collaboration

    Our senior editor and project manager will carefully build your first draft around your specific preferences. When ready, we’ll upload it to Frame.io so you can add direct comments and annotations right on the timeline. From there, we’ll implement your feedback to polish every detail.

  4. Finalization

    After incorporating your feedback, we’ll upload the polished cut to Frame.io for your final review. Once approved, we will lock the edit and bundle all project assets for export.

  5. Project handover

    After your final sign-off, we’ll send a PayPal payment link. As soon as payment is complete, we’ll deliver your high-resolution exports along with all project files and assets directly via Dropbox.

Studios we edit for

    Good to know

    How does a project work?

    It starts when you get in touch, by email, WhatsApp or the brief form. We chat about your project, and a short creative brief captures your preferences. We build a first draft, share it on Frame.io for your comments on the timeline, polish it with your feedback, and do a final review. After sign-off, we hand over your files.

    What can you edit?

    Teasers, highlight films, full-length films, and ceremony and speeches edits. If you need something else, ask and we’ll tell you if it’s a fit.

    How do I send you the footage?

    Share a download link (Dropbox, Google Drive or similar) in the brief, or send it when we reply. If your files are large or messy, tell us and we’ll work out the easiest way to get them over.

    Can you match my editing style?

    Yes. Choose “Match my existing style” in the brief and send a few of your previous films or references. If you’d rather explore, we can go cinematic or documentary, or help you decide.

    Can you use the songs I choose?

    Commercial songs are copyrighted and need a license to be used in a final film. Any songs you share are used as inspiration for mood and pacing, and we find licensed music in a similar style. You can also pick a genre and mood in the brief, or let us choose.

    How long will it take, and what does it cost?

    Turnaround depends on your deadline and how much footage there is. We confirm your exact price and timing within a day of receiving your brief.

    How do reviews and revisions work?

    We upload drafts to Frame.io, where you can leave comments directly on the timeline. Revisions are unlimited: we apply your feedback as many times as needed, then send the polished cut for a final review before we lock the edit.

    How do payment and delivery work?

    After your final sign-off we send a PayPal payment link. Once payment is complete, we deliver your high-resolution exports, project files and assets via Dropbox.

    Where are you based?

    We’re a collaborative team working together in a studio in Manila, Philippines.

    What to have ready

    You don’t need everything on day one. The more you share, the faster we can start.

    • Footage link

      A Dropbox, Google Drive or similar download link. Tell us if the files are large or messy.

    • Your dates

      The wedding date and the date you need the film by.

    • The deliverable

      Teaser, highlights, full film, or ceremony and speeches.

    • Style references

      A few films you like, or your own earlier work if you want us to match it.

    • Music and notes

      A music genre and mood (or let us choose), any songs for inspiration, plus must-keep moments and names we should know.

    Privacy

    We keep this simple: we only collect what we need to answer you and do the work.

    What we collect

    The details you enter in the brief form (name, email, dates, project details and footage link), or anything you send us by email or chat.

    Why

    To reply to your inquiry and, if you hire us, to deliver your project. We don’t sell your details or use them for third-party marketing.

    Who handles it

    Briefs reach us by email, either through your own email app or our form service. Emails are handled through Gmail. Footage you share is used only to edit your project.

    Analytics

    We use Plausible, a privacy-friendly analytics tool that doesn’t use cookies or collect personal data, to count visits and clicks.

    On your device

    If you pick a light or dark theme, that choice is saved in your browser only. So is a half-finished brief, until you send it. Fonts come from Google Fonts, and film thumbnails and players come from YouTube. Both may receive your device information when they load or play.

    Your choices

    Email marcusweddingstudio@gmail.com any time to ask us to correct or delete your details.
