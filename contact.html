Terms and conditions

These terms keep things clear for both of us. By sending a brief, you agree to them.

Quotes and pricing

Prices on this site are starting prices. We confirm your exact price and timing by email before we start work, and nothing is charged before you agree to it.

Your footage and music

You confirm you have the right to use the footage and any music you send us. Commercial songs need a license. If you don’t have a music account, we can provide licensed music from Musicbed. Please keep your own copy of your original footage.

Timeline

Typical turnaround is 3–4 days for a teaser and highlight film, and 4–5 days if we also edit the ceremony and speeches. It is counted from the day we receive your footage and complete details. Late or incomplete footage moves the delivery date.

Reviews and revisions

Every project includes 2–3 rounds of revisions on the agreed brief, reviewed on Frame.io. Extra rounds or changes to the brief are agreed with you first and may cost extra.

Rush and test edits

Rush delivery carries an additional fee, confirmed before we start. A 30–60 second test edit carries a small fee, confirmed when we reply.

Payment and delivery

You pay after you approve the final edit, through a PayPal payment link. Once it is paid, we deliver the high-resolution exports and project files through Dropbox.

Privacy of your work

Your footage is used only to edit your project. We don’t share your footage or finished films publicly without your permission.

Questions

Email marcusweddingstudio@gmail.com before you send your brief if anything here doesn’t work for you.

Privacy

We keep this simple: we only collect what we need to answer you and do the work.

What we collect

The details you enter in the brief form (name, email, dates, project details and footage link), or anything you send us by email or chat.

Why

To reply to your inquiry and, if you hire us, to deliver your project. We don’t sell your details or use them for third-party marketing.

Who handles it

Briefs reach us by email, either through your own email app or through our form service (Formspree). Emails are handled through Gmail. Footage you share is used only to edit your project.

Analytics

We use Plausible, a privacy-friendly analytics tool that doesn’t use cookies or collect personal data, to count visits and clicks.

On your device

If you pick a light or dark theme, that choice is saved in your browser only. So is a half-finished brief, until you send it. Fonts come from Google Fonts, and film thumbnails and players come from YouTube. Both may receive your device information when they load or play.

Your choices

Email marcusweddingstudio@gmail.com any time to ask us to correct or delete your details.
