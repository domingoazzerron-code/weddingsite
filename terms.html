1. Quotes and pricing

Prices on our pricing page are starting prices. Your exact price depends on your footage and deadline. We confirm both by email within a day of receiving your brief, and we don’t start work until you agree to them. Nothing is charged before you agree.

2. What is included

  • An edit matched to your brief, style references and music direction.
  • 2–3 rounds of revisions on the agreed brief, reviewed on Frame.io.
  • High-resolution exports and project files delivered by Dropbox after payment.

3. What can cost extra

We confirm each of these with you before we do the work:

  • Rush delivery, when you need the film faster than our usual turnaround.
  • Extra revision rounds, or changes to the agreed brief.
  • Changing the music after the edit has started, because it means re-editing.
  • A 30–60 second test edit, which carries a small fee confirmed when we reply.

4. Your footage and music

You confirm that you have the right to use the footage and any music you send us. Commercial songs need a license. If you have your own music account, send us the track and we’ll use it. If you don’t, we can provide licensed music from Musicbed in a similar style. Please keep your own copy of your original footage until the project is delivered.

5. Timeline

Typical turnaround is 3–4 days for a teaser and highlight film, and 4–5 days if we also edit the ceremony and speeches. It is counted from the day we receive your footage and complete details. Timing also depends on your deadline, and we plan around the date you give us. Late or incomplete footage moves the delivery date, and we’ll tell you the new date as soon as we know.

6. Reviews and revisions

Your first draft is shared on Frame.io. Leave comments on the timeline, or send plain notes, and we update the cut each round. Every project includes 2–3 rounds on the agreed brief. If you need more rounds or want to change the brief, we agree that with you first.

7. Payment and delivery

You pay after you approve the final edit, through a PayPal payment link we send you. Once it is paid, we deliver the high-resolution exports and all project files through Dropbox.

8. Ownership and privacy of your work

You own your project files. We edit and deliver the agreed film files for you and your couple, and we don’t claim ownership of your project. Your footage is used only to edit your project. We don’t share your footage or finished films publicly, or use them in our own marketing, without your permission. See also our privacy notice, linked in the footer of our main pages.
