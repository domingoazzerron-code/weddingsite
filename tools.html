Tools

The tools behind every film we edit, from your raw footage to the finished delivery.

Edit & sound

Editing

Adobe Premiere Pro

Our main editing software. Every film is cut, colour-matched and finished here.

  • Multicam sync for ceremonies and speeches shot on several cameras
  • Lumetri colour to match different cameras and set the final look
  • Proxy files, so heavy 4K footage still edits smoothly
Audio

Adobe Podcast

We use its Enhance Speech tool to clean up vows, speeches and toasts.

  • Reduces echo, wind and background noise from on-camera or phone audio
  • Cleaned audio goes back into Premiere and is balanced with the music
  • Every spoken word stays clear and easy to hear
Music

Musicbed

Licensed music for your teaser and highlights, picked to match the mood of your day.

  • Have your own music account? Tell us and we will use it
  • No account? We provide properly licensed music for you
  • Tracks are chosen with your creative brief in mind

Review, delivery & planning

Review

Frame.io

Where you watch and review your first draft online.

  • Leave comments on the exact frame you mean
  • Revisions come back as new versions you can compare
  • Works in any browser, nothing to install
Delivery

Dropbox

How footage comes in and finished films go out.

  • Send your raw footage with a shared Dropbox link
  • Large video files move without email size limits
  • Your final film is delivered here after payment
Planning

Trello

One board keeps every wedding on schedule.

  • A card for each project, with deadline and notes
  • Stages follow our workflow, from consultation to handover
  • Nothing gets lost between footage received and final delivery

Privacy

We keep this simple: we only collect what we need to answer you and do the work.

What we collect

The details you enter in the brief form (name, email, dates, project details and footage link), or anything you send us by email or chat.

Why

To reply to your inquiry and, if you hire us, to deliver your project. We don’t sell your details or use them for third-party marketing.

Who handles it

Briefs reach us by email, either through your own email app or through our form service (Formspree). Emails are handled through Gmail. Footage you share is used only to edit your project.

Analytics

We use Plausible, a privacy-friendly analytics tool that doesn’t use cookies or collect personal data, to count visits and clicks.

On your device

If you pick a light or dark theme, that choice is saved in your browser only. So is a half-finished brief, until you send it. Fonts come from Google Fonts, and film thumbnails and players come from YouTube. Both may receive your device information when they load or play.

Your choices

Email marcusweddingstudio@gmail.com any time to ask us to correct or delete your details.
